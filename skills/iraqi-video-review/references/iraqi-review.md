# Iraqi review protocol — v0.2, 2026-10-10

## Scope

This is an original creative-review framework, informed by the linked research repositories. Its weights are explicit editorial choices, not coefficients learned from Iraqi audiences. Ratings are not probabilities. Never represent the repositories' models, global datasets or LLM-generated percentages as Iraqi validation.

## Project context

Keep the project's actual brand and audience. Request or leave unknown: platform, organic or paid, goal (awareness / qualified messages / leads / sales), Iraqi segment, city/region, factual offer, relevant evidence, reference footage and reviewed speech. A folder name does not establish the business, course contents or audience. Use Iraqi Arabic where specified, with natural wording suited to the audience; do not manufacture dialect, imply uniform Iraqi tastes, or invent buying behavior.

## Available evidence

Sample the opening (0, 0.75, 1.5, 3 seconds, clamped for short clips), middle/end and clip boundaries. Prefer the current rendered export so burned-in text and placement are visible. If only planned source frames are available, label the result accordingly; a draft overlay is not observed rendered text. Cite the image/time IDs for every scored criterion. Identify technical audio-track/volume facts separately. Volume does not establish intelligibility, words or emotional tone. Full spoken/audio assessment requires listening and reviewed transcription. User-provided proposed copy is not a transcript.

## Editorial rubric

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence to inspect |
|---|---:|---|
| Opening hook | 25 | Visible relevant promise, problem, result or curiosity with truthful context |
| Relevant value | 20 | A clear benefit or takeaway for the stated viewer |
| Iraqi audience fit | 15 | Specific supplied segment/location, suitable language, relevant situation; unknown if segment absent |
| Visual/text clarity | 15 | Readable text, framing, brand/product visibility and safe placement in sampled frames |
| Proof and trust | 10 | A visible demonstration or verified fact supporting the promise; no invented social proof |
| Appropriate CTA | 10 | Next action suits awareness, qualified inquiry, leads or purchase goal |
| Spoken/audio clarity | 5 | Must be heard; technical volume alone leaves this criterion unknown |

Grade each 0–5, or unknown. Compute the weighted average only over known evidence-backed criteria. Display known-weight coverage separately; do not show an aggregate below 50% coverage. The local implementation always leaves audio clarity unknown because it does not listen. Coverage is evidence availability, not model confidence. Good video structure does not imply reach, orders or conversion probability.

Suggest at most three timed edits and three genuinely different hook angles. Preserve the project's approved facts, audio and identity. Sales reviews prioritize qualified action and proof; awareness reviews can consider useful sharing and saving. Do not insert fake price, delivery, COD, offer, urgency, testimonial, creator data or trend. Never require a universal video length, cut frequency or posting time. Recommendations remain hypotheses until tested.

## Outcome snapshots and comparisons

Store exact export/post identifiers, platform, distribution, objective, segment/location and snapshot age. Baghdad publication time is separate from the measurement age. Report views, shares, saves, qualified messages/leads/orders, spend and attributed revenue as actually supplied. Unknown values remain null. IQD is the project's reporting currency; it is not inferred price copy.

Use shares/saves/qualified outcomes per 1000 views as diagnostics. Where verified cost and outcomes exist, report IQD cost per qualified result; report ROAS only when revenue and positive spend are known. Views-per-follower can contextualize scale but do not forecast reach from follower count. Watch time may exceed duration on loops. Repeat snapshots of the same post/platform/age replace previous values. Retain different ages separately.

Compare only records with matching project, posting account, platform, paid/organic, objective, actual audience segment/location and snapshot age. If the segment or posting account is unknown, no cohort summary. Medians and record counts are descriptive; no automatic winner or stop/scale threshold. Check spend distribution, sample size, attribution and downstream quality before campaign decisions.

## Before a trained Iraqi predictor

Collect representative Iraqi posts, including nonviral and low-performance videos, not only top-trending winners. Fix outcome definitions and observation ages. Separate creators/projects and chronological future periods across training/validation/test; keep duplicate footage/creative variants together. Fit preprocessing only on training data. Use pre-publication features for prediction, never post-publication likes/shares/views as input. Separate paid/organic outcomes and account exposure. Compare to simple baselines and validate on future Iraqi samples. Only then estimate calibration and uncertainty; report the actual validation sample, error metrics and population. Current Studio v0.2 has no trained/calibrated predictor.
