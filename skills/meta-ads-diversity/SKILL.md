# Meta Ads Creative Diversity

For Studio production, first read [the execution contract](../sadeq-studio/references/execution.md). These instructions are packaged with Sadeq Studio; sibling production/review skills are included.

## Brief and evidence

Use the current product files, existing brief, previous decisions in the task, and performance data before asking questions. Identify the offer, verified benefits, buying motivations, audience, objective, available footage, and production constraints. Ask only for missing information that changes the deliverable; continue independent work with labeled assumptions. Use Iraqi Arabic for Iraqi-market copy unless the user specifies otherwise.

Keep product facts local to the current brief. Never transfer a previous product's price, warranty, compatibility, or delivery offer to a new product. Do not invent reviews, results, scarcity, demonstrations, or capabilities. Separate supplied claims, observed evidence, and unverified assumptions.

## Develop distinct concepts

Treat a concept as a persuasive idea: a buying motivation plus a message, evidence, and presentation. Select useful differences across needs, use situations, awareness levels, objections, hooks, presenters, and formats. Examples include a product demonstration, a problem and solution, an evidenced comparison, an objection answer, or an authentic customer story when a real story exists.

Vary the persuasive reason and visual opening across a concept batch. Color swaps, font changes, synonyms, and different durations of the same story are execution variants, not separate concepts. Label them accurately. Do not require an arbitrary number of differences or ads; fit the batch to the user's request, available assets, and testing budget.

For each requested video concept, provide a concise concept ID, intended viewer or motivation, angle, hook, script, shot plan, on-screen text, proof, CTA, and production notes as useful. For stills or copy-only tasks, adapt to the requested medium. Use a compact comparison table when it helps explain how concepts differ; do not force a full campaign plan onto a small edit.

Explain why each concept is worth testing as a hypothesis, not a prediction of guaranteed performance. Flag missing footage rather than claiming it exists. For actual video production, also read `../arabic-product-video/SKILL.md`.

## Andromeda: evidence boundary

Andromeda is Meta's internal personalized ad retrieval system, one stage in its recommendation pipeline. It is not a ChatGPT mode, a switch this skill can activate, or an advertiser-controlled algorithm. Distinguish it from Advantage+ settings and subsequent ranking systems.

Use creative diversity as a practical strategy to explore different customer motivations. Do not attribute fixed ad counts, spend thresholds, refresh schedules, similarity penalties, targeting rules, or guaranteed ROAS to Andromeda without current primary evidence. Do not claim Meta has evaluated the semantic diversity of an asset unless an actual tool or report provides that result.

Baseline source, published 2024-12-02 and reviewed 2026-10-06:
https://engineering.fb.com/2024/12/02/production-engineering/meta-andromeda-advantage-automation-next-gen-personalized-ads-retrieval-engine/

Check current official Meta documentation when recommending changing campaign controls, current specs, or platform policies. Cite the source and distinguish Meta's statements from your strategic inference. This skill itself does not connect a Meta account or install video tools.

## Test and learn

Distinguish concept exploration, where several creative elements may change together, from a controlled test designed to isolate one variable. Name the hypothesis and business outcome. Fit the metric to the objective: purchase acquisition cost and revenue for sales, qualified leads for lead generation, or qualified conversations for messaging. Use viewing and click metrics as diagnostics rather than automatic proof of sales effectiveness.

When reviewing results, check spend, sample size, attribution window, delivery imbalance, audience and placement differences, dates, and downstream quality before declaring a winner. If data is insufficient, say what can and cannot be concluded. Avoid universal stop/scale thresholds and fixed numbers of days. Recommend next concepts from observed results while preserving successful ideas for controlled iteration.

Deliver requested creative assets and a proportionate testing plan. Drafting and rendering do not authorize publication, budget changes, or external uploads; act only within the user's actual authorization.
