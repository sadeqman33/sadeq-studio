# Speech takes

Read [the execution contract](../sadeq-studio/references/execution.md) first to resolve the existing runtime/project and preserve independent works. Read the relevant source context before proposing edits. Run speech-picks --project ID --asset ID, or read speech_picks in selected context. Use surrounding ASR segments/word times and source-time lineage. Repetition/restart cues and recognizer metrics are hypotheses, not listening or proof the speaker made a mistake. Do not retranscribe unchanged footage only to get picks.

Compare complete alternatives for meaning, continuity and delivery. Keep negation, conditions, prices, names, quantities, promises and uncertainty. Prefer a complete natural take to an abandoned start only when the content agrees. Conflicting prices/facts need evidence; a fluency score cannot decide truth. Deliberate pauses, dialect markers and emphasis may belong in the edit. Never stitch syllables into new dialogue or remove context to alter a claim.

Listen to important ambiguous ranges with available audio tools and mark any listening still needed. Place trim margins around words where source allows, then check each join. Explain kept/dropped take ranges in reason and retain originals/history. Read timeline-plan.md for translating SOURCE times into contiguous editable trims. Overlay text is a separate decision; do not label rewritten ad copy a verbatim transcript.
